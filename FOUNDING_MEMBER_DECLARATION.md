# Founding Member Declaration

**This is not a legal document.** It's written in plain language, by the
founder, so you know exactly what you're joining and what you're not
agreeing to. It will be replaced by a proper Terms of Service once
TheraNet Technologies is formally registered and that document has been
through counsel — see the note at the bottom for why that hasn't happened
yet.

## What AHP Network is, right now

AHP Network is a small, invite-only pilot for physiotherapists,
occupational therapists, and speech-language pathologists in Hyderabad. It's
operated by TheraNet Technologies — at the time you're reading this, not yet
a formally registered company. You're one of a small founding cohort, not a
customer of an established platform.

## What you're agreeing to by using it

- You're joining as a **founding member** of a pilot, not a finished
  product. Things will change, sometimes quickly, based on what the
  cohort needs.
- The information you provide (your profile, your credentials, referrals
  you post or accept) is used to run the platform as described in the
  [Interim Data & Privacy Notice](./INTERIM_PRIVACY_NOTICE.md) — read that
  alongside this one.
- **"Credentials Verified" and "Qualification Confirmed" mean exactly what
  their tooltips say on your profile, and nothing more.** An admin checked
  that a document you uploaded looks consistent with what you claimed. It
  is not a clinical endorsement, not a guarantee of your current council
  registration, not a recommendation, and not an assessment of the quality
  of your care.
- **AHP Network is not a healthcare provider.** It doesn't employ or
  supervise you, isn't a party to any care relationship you enter into
  through a referral, and doesn't arrange, route, or take responsibility
  for patient care. Referrals made through the platform are between you
  and the other professional.
- Because this is a small, early pilot, the platform may have bugs, gaps,
  or downtime. There's no service-level guarantee at this stage — if
  something breaks, tell the founder directly (see below) and it'll get
  fixed as fast as a very small team can manage.
- You can ask to have your account and data removed at any time. See the
  Interim Data & Privacy Notice for what "removed" actually means for
  each piece of data.

## Why this document exists instead of a real ToS

TheraNet Technologies is deliberately staying unregistered, and formal
legal counsel is deliberately not yet engaged, for as long as this stays a
small, unmonetized Hyderabad pilot. That's a founder decision, not an
oversight — spending on registration and counsel is being held until
there's a real reason to spend it: expanding beyond Hyderabad, or turning
on monetization. Neither has happened yet.

This declaration and its companion privacy notice are the honest,
temporary bridge for that in-between period. They'll be replaced in full,
not patched, once the real documents exist.
